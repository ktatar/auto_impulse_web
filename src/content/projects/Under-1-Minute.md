Under 1 Minute (U1M) is an audio-visual performance project that explores the notion of a collective artistic mind. The artificial intelligence character “The One” joins three human artists, Mehmet Ünal, Kıvanç Tatar, and Hakan Yılmaz. The AI in U1M learns from human artists to represent collective artistic aesthetics. U1M takes the audience for an experiential journey of audio-visual performance through its unique narrative and aesthetics.
